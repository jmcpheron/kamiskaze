Video Player Changes Verification

What You Should See:

  1. Audio/Video Mode Buttons - Above the album art, you should see two buttons:
    • "Audio" button (active by default, with orange border)
    • "Video" button (click to switch to video mode)
  2. When in Video Mode:
    • Album art disappears
    • Black video player appears with controls at the bottom
    • Controls auto-hide after 3 seconds when playing

Quick Test:

Open your browser and go to: http://localhost:8002

If you don't see the Audio/Video buttons, try:

  1. Hard refresh: Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) or Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows)
  2. Open in Incognito/Private mode to bypass cache
  3. Clear browser cache for localhost
