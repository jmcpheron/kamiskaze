    About Kamiskaze

    SoCal Ska Band Archives

    The Band

    Kamiskaze was a Southern California ska band that captured the energy and spirit of the mid-90s ska revival scene. Known for their high-energy performances and tight musicianship, the band was part of the vibrant SoCal ska community that helped define the third wave ska movement.

    The band performed at various venues throughout Southern California, including memorable shows at The Jawas in Palm Springs, where these recordings were captured in 1996.

    The Archives

    This collection preserves live recordings and audio tracks from Kamiskaze's performances, digitized and made available for fans and ska enthusiasts. The recordings showcase the raw energy of live ska performances from the height of the ska revival era.

    Audio tracks have been carefully preserved and are presented here both as streaming audio and, where available, as video recordings from live performances.
