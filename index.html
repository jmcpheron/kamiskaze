About

Kamiskaze was a Southern California ska band that captured the energy and spirit of the mid-90s ska revival scene. Known for their high-energy performances and tight musicianship, the band was part of the vibrant SoCal ska community that helped define the third wave ska movement.

The band performed at various venues throughout Orange County and LA County, including The Showcase Theater in Corona, The Roxy, and memorable shows at The Jawas in Palm Springs, where these recordings were captured in 1996.
